# PoiBuilder v0.9.192

Changes since **v0.9.186**, the previous release. This is still an **alpha**.

Download `PoiBuilder-v0.9.192.zip` below and import it through Godot's
**AssetLib → Import…**, or extract it at your project root. Then enable the
plugin in **Project → Project Settings → Plugins**.

## Before you upgrade

- **Your v0.9.186 and v0.9.167 maps keep working.** They load, render and
  paint exactly as before. Both are automated tests against scenes those
  releases wrote themselves. v0.9.186's full demo map also reads back
  identically: every mesh, face, paint mask and decal, byte for byte.
- **The stock water and tile textures look different.** The waterfall sheet
  and core, water pool, water foam and the light and wet floor tiles were
  redrawn (a streaky falling curtain instead of blotches, caustics, mottled
  tiles). They keep their names, sizes and layout, so maps that use them load
  unchanged and simply show the new look. To keep the old look, copy the old
  PNGs out of v0.9.186 into your own project before you upgrade and point
  your materials at the copies.
- **Your editor layout stays as it is.** Earlier versions moved the
  Inspector, Node and Groups docks into a second right-hand column on every
  load. This version never moves your docks, but a layout the old version
  already changed stays as Godot saved it. Drag the Inspector back once if you
  want one column again.

## Highlights

- **A Material & UV dock that fits.** It is now a regular Godot dock: below
  the Inspector in a fresh layout, and you can move it to any slot, float it,
  or put it in the bottom panel, where the palette and the tools sit side by
  side. It works down to about 200 px wide; the six modes become a row of icon
  buttons (hover one for its name). The palette gets a filter box, a card-size
  slider (also Ctrl+mouse wheel over the palette) and a draggable split
  between the palette and the tools. The dock's position, card size and split
  are remembered.
- **Edge-first shape placement (optional).** Click a start point, click the
  end of the first edge at any angle (it sets the shape's rotation and width),
  pull out the depth (Shift for a square), then the height. Good for walls
  that do not follow the world axes. Turn it on with the **Placement**
  dropdown in the dock's Shapes mode, or in **Editor Settings → PoiBuilder →
  Creation → Placement Style**. The usual drag stays the default.
- **Animated retro maps.** A `.pbm` can now carry hardware vertex morphing
  (travelling waves, swaying palms) at no per-vertex CPU cost on the PSP,
  mesh flipbooks, additive and fog-free meshes, decal overlays, the map's own
  sky and fog, and a camera path that the PSP app flies in attract mode
  (12 s idle, or Select+R). They are authored as node metadata; the docs'
  Export page lists them and the format spec defines them. A loader that does
  not know them draws the map static. The new **seashore** demo map uses all
  of them and runs at 85+ fps on a real PSP in every view we measured.
- **Updated documentation.** New screenshots and the new showcase film, and
  pages for the new dock, edge-first placement and runtime effects.

## Fixed

- **Scenes, meshes and textures can be dragged into the 3D view again.**
  With the plugin enabled, only materials could be dropped into the viewport.
  Material drops now also work in every viewport of the 2/3/4-view layouts.
- **A stamp placed at a corner no longer paints a smeared copy onto a nearby
  slanted face** (in the editor and in exports). A stamp now paints only
  inside its own footprint. It still wraps a chamfer or bevel continuously.
  Copies that already bled are pixels and stay; rub them out with the decal
  brush's Erase.
- **Shift+drag extrude of a big face follows the gizmo.** Dragging a door's
  front face 1 m out could extrude only ~0.6 m when the camera was close.
- **The N-Gon tool's "Height" readout goes away** when you confirm or cancel
  (it stayed on screen until the next shape).
- The placement hints said "Select the Material & UV tab to exit". They now
  name the **Material** mode button.

**PSP demo engine**
- Cutout textures (tree leaves) no longer show a white halo against the sky.
- Each mesh is frustum-culled: 0.85–2.0 ms saved per view on an open map.
- The light bake no longer runs out of memory on a sky dome (it took 5 min
  and 2 GB; now ~2 s).
- The `depth_write` render knob was reversed. It now means what it says, and
  the format spec describes what the runtime really does (depth is written).

## Known issues

- **A stamp cannot span two objects.** It covers several faces of one mesh,
  but stops at object boundaries. Workaround: merge the objects first.
- **Trim Walls at Top placement around doors and arches** does not produce
  one continuous cornice. Use Bottom placement for loops with openings (see
  the Trims page).
- **Shift+drag on a face of a Poibuilderized GLB prop** can report
  "degenerate region normal" and move the face instead of extruding it.
